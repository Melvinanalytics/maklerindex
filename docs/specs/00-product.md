# Product

Finde den Makler, nicht das Portal.

## Audience

German Eigentümer hiring a listing agent. Not buyers. Not searchers of Wohnungen.

## Money

None on the index. No ads on ranking. No lead fees. No affiliate. A later claim must not boost rank.

## Architecture law

OKF v0.2 is the product. HTML, `llms.txt`, and MCP are projections of `okf/`.

## v1

Hannover DEMO. Three owner routes: `/`, `/hannover/`, `/hannover/<slug>/`. Outbound only. UTM on website links only. mailto and tel stay clean. No form. DEMO labeled. Contacts are `example.invalid`.

## Out of scope

Buyer search. Listing portal. OpenImmo. IS24. PropStack. Payments. Custom domain. Brand lock.
